# Verge AI — LLM-Optimized Site Content

## Company Profile
Verge AI (蔚科融智（深圳）科技有限公司 / Verge AI Limited) is an edge AI company founded 2026-03-26 with offices in Shenzhen and Hong Kong. The company develops AI hardware and software that runs entirely on-device, spanning enterprise appliances, consumer home devices, and commercial robotics.

## Product Line Summary

### Enterprise
- **VergeOps AI Appliance**: Air-gapped private AI for IT operations — root cause analysis, smart ticketing, SLA guardian, security autonomous defense. Runs 70B–405B models on-prem.

### Consumer Edge AI Home (Hub + Satellite)
- **Edge AI Home Hub**: Central brain with RK3588 (6 TOPS NPU), NVMe NAS (512GB–2TB), transparent 17" OLED display, Zigbee 3.0, WebSocket Agent Protocol.
- **AI Smart Mirror**: 12"–15" semi-transparent mirror with skin analysis, AR makeup try-on, daily health quick-check. RK3588 + ESP32-S3.
- **AI Kitchen Agent**: 7"–10" IPS touchscreen with dual-camera (RGB + NIR) food scanning, voice control, 10,000+ recipe database. RK3588.
- **AI Study Buddy**: 6"–8" touchscreen desktop tutor with long-term memory, emotion sensing (voice tone + expression), Qwen3-2B SLM.
- **AI Pet Buddy**: 4K PTZ camera with starlight CMOS + IR night vision, animal behavior AI, multi-pet facial recognition (up to 5 pets).
- **AI Elderly Buddy**: 4" circular LCD desktop companion with 60GHz mmWave radar fall detection, rPPG health monitoring, dialect support (Cantonese/Mandarin/Sichuanese).

### Robotics
- **Verge Carry**: Autonomous delivery robot, 50kg payload, 4h+ battery.
- **Verge Scrub**: Commercial floor scrubber, 2,000 m²/h coverage, 3h runtime.

## Technical Highlights
- Edge AI chips: RK3588 (6 TOPS NPU), optional Jetson Orin NX (100 TOPS)
- SLM models: Qwen3-2B/4B W8A8 @ NPU (11.5 t/s per device)
- Speech: SenseVoice Small ASR (~150ms) + CosyVoice TTS (~300ms)
- Agent Protocol: WebSocket-based encrypted local communication
- Privacy: Physical camera shutters on all camera-equipped devices
- Data storage: Local SQLite + Hub NVMe NAS (AES-256 + TPM)
