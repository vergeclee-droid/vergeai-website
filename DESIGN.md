## Overview

Verge AI's website communicates a brand that sits at the intersection of **frontier technology and consumer trust**. The visual language is inspired by companies like Cohere and Linear — spacious, type-forward, and resolutely minimal. Every design decision reinforces the core message: edge intelligence that is private, secure, and real-time.

The site is trilingual (English / 简体中文 / 繁體中文), targeting a dual audience of enterprise IT buyers and consumer early-adopters across Greater China and global markets. The design does not try to impress with chrome, gradients, or illustrations. Instead, it lets the product narrative and the generous typographic scale do the work.

## Brand & Style

The brand personality is **innovative but grounded** — premium in feel, not in price. The emotional register is calm confidence, not hype.

The hero lands on a single massive "Verge AI" wordmark (300 weight, 5.5rem) centred above a near-white gradient background. There is no stock photography, no animated hero video, no complex illustration. The logo mark carries the brand identity alone.

## Colors

The palette is intentionally restrained — a cool-neutral base punctuated by a single cyan accent and a secondary indigo-purple shift for AI signalling.

### Core palette

- **Near-black (#17171c):** The anchor. Used for hero text, primary buttons, nav CTA, and footer. Slightly softer than true black (#000).
- **White (#ffffff):** Background canvas for light sections, card surfaces, and content areas.
- **Slate-50 → Slate-900:** A 10-stop cool grey scale. Body text lives at `slate-600`. Headings at `near-black`. Section headers use the full range.

### Accent palette

- **Blue (#06b6d4):** Single interaction accent. Used for metric badges, hover border glows, section tag backgrounds, and link highlights.
- **Indigo (#818cf8):** Secondary accent. Appears in use-case card hover borders and purple metric badges.
- **Purple (#a78bfa):** Used in the purple-mist section background (`#f5f3ff`).
- **Purple-mist (#f5f3ff):** Alternating section background for the B2C / Edge AI Home section.

### Section backgrounds

- **Light sections:** White (`#ffffff`).
- **Dark sections (B2B - VergeOps, Robotics):** `slate-800` (`#1e293b`), with white text and muted slate-300 body copy.
- **Mist sections (B2C - Edge AI Home):** `purple-mist` (`#f5f3ff`).
- **Hero background:** Linear gradient `slate-50 → white → purple-mist`.

### Section transition system

- **Dark to Light:** An 80px gradient (slate-900 → transparent) applied via `::after` on the dark section.
- **Light to Mist:** An 80px gradient (transparent → purple-mist) applied via `::before` on the mist section.

## Typography

Verge AI uses a **two-family type system** inspired by Cohere:

- **Space Grotesk (Display):** All headings (h1-h5), section tags, scroll indicator, footer tagline. Always used with light weight (300-400), tight letter-spacing (-0.03em to -0.04em), and minimal line-height (1.1).
- **Inter (Body):** Body text, card descriptions, metric labels, buttons, and navigation. Used at 400-500 weight with standard line-height (1.5-1.6).

### Type scale philosophy

The site uses `clamp()` for responsive type sizing. Headings are intentionally **large and light** — the h1 at 5.5rem (300 weight) is the dominant visual element on the page, but it never feels heavy. This creates a distinctive editorial feel that separates Verge AI from conventional tech sites.

- **h1:** The Verge AI wordmark. Massive (`5.5rem`), ultra-light (`300`), tight tracking (`-0.04em`). Never bold.
- **h2:** Section titles. Large (`3.5rem`) but light (`400`). Subtitle line uses Inter at `1.1rem`, regular weight.
- **h3:** Sub-section titles in body regions. Inter, `1.6rem`, 500 weight.
- **h4, h5:** Card titles and product labels. Space Grotesk, `0.9-1.1rem`, 400 weight.
- **Tagline (hero):** Space Grotesk, `1.6rem`, 300 weight. The secondary headline.
- **Subtitle (hero):** Space Grotesk, `0.85rem`, uppercase, letter-spaced. Company registration line.

### Chinese type

All Chinese runs through the same font stack: `Space Grotesk, Inter, Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans TC`. CJK characters are handled by the Noto Sans fonts at the end of the fallback chain. Type scale and spacing are shared between scripts.

## Layout

The layout is **max-width centred single-column**.

- **Container:** `1200px` max-width, `32px` horizontal padding.
- **Section padding:** `clamp(80px, 12vh, 140px)` vertical.
- **Content width:** Body text is constrained to `680px` max-width. Card grids use the full container width.

### Page flow

1. **Nav** (fixed, translucent white, 60px) → **Hero** (full viewport, centred) → **About/Mission** (light) → **VergeOps** (dark) → **Robotics** (dark) → **Edge AI Home** (mist) → **Technology** (light) → **Contact** (light) → **Footer** (near-black).

### Grid patterns

- **Mission grid:** 3 columns — pillars of the brand value proposition.
- **Use cases (VergeOps):** 2 columns — product features with metric badges.
- **Pillars (Edge AI Home):** Flex row, 3 items max 360px — product category overview.
- **Products row:** 4 columns — individual product cards.
- **Tech strip:** 4 columns — technology capability cards.
- **Contact grid:** 2 columns — office location cards.

All grids collapse to single column on mobile (`768px` breakpoint).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is minimal — almost flat.

- **Cards:** No shadow. Depth is created by a white surface on the slightly off-white background (`slate-100` border).
- **Navbar:** Translucent white with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`. Gained a `1px slate-100` bottom border on scroll.
- **Hover:** `translateY(-2px)` + border colour shift to accent colour. No shadow increase, no lift.
- **Footer:** `near-black` background — the only high-elevation visual element.

No blur except navbar backdrop. No drop shadows on cards. No glassmorphism.

## Shapes

- **Cards (mission, use-case, pillar, tech, contact):** 22px — generous rounding that softens the cool palette.
- **Product cards, mobile CTA buttons:** 14px.
- **Buttons and nav CTA:** Fully rounded (9999px) pill shape.
- **Section tags, metric badges:** Fully rounded pills.
- **Language switcher buttons:** 6px — subtle, not pill-shaped.

## Animations

Extremely restrained:

- **Card hover:** `0.3s all` — only border colour and translateY change.
- **Button hover:** `0.25s` — background darkening + `translateY(-1px)` on primary.
- **Hamburger menu:** `0.35s` opacity transition on mobile menu overlay.
- **Scroll indicator:** `2s ease-in-out infinite` float animation on hero.
- **Navbar background:** `0.3s` ease transition on scroll.
- **No scroll-triggered reveals.** No parallax. No loading animations.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: all transforms and opacity changes are set to `none` and `auto`.

## Negative space

White space is a deliberate design element. The hero has generous bottom padding. Cards within grids are separated by 24px gaps. Sections get `12vh` vertical padding. The type scale's light weight and open tracking keep even dense sections feeling airy.

## Language System

Trilingual content is handled through `data-lang` attributes:
- `data-lang="en"` — English
- `data-lang="zh-hans"` — Simplified Chinese
- `data-lang="zh-hant"` — Traditional Chinese

CSS toggles `display: none/block` based on the `html[lang]` attribute. JavaScript `switchLang()` function updates the lang attribute, stores preference in `localStorage`, and changes the `<html lang>` attribute.

## Product catalogue pages

Each Edge AI Home product (Smart Mirror, Home Hub, Kitchen Agent, Study Buddy, Pet Buddy, Elderly Buddy) has a standalone catalogue page under `/catalogue-{product}.html`. These pages follow the same visual language: centered layout, light type weight, generous padding, cyan accent, and minimal chrome.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** keep headings in Space Grotesk at 300-400 weight. Never use bold on display headings.
- **Do** use the near-black (`#17171c`) for primary buttons, nav CTA, and strong emphasis. Do not use pure black (#000).
- **Do** use `data-lang` for all visible text — no hardcoded monolingual content.
- **Do** use `touch-action: manipulation` on all interactive elements to eliminate 300ms tap delay.
- **Don't** use drop shadows on cards. Depth comes from border contrast and hover translateY.
- **Don't** use gradient backgrounds anywhere except the hero and section transitions. No card gradients, no button gradients.
- **Don't** use stock photography, illustrations, or decorative imagery. The logo and emoji icons are the entire visual vocabulary.
- **Don't** use serif fonts in any role.
- **Don't** introduce additional accent colours. The blue/indigo/purple triad is the entire accent palette.
- **Don't** animate on scroll, use parallax, or implement complex loading sequences.
- **Don't** round nav links. Only the CTA button gets pill rounding.
- **Do** respect `prefers-color-scheme: dark` — provide a basic dark fallback for the body background.
- **Do** respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — collapse all animations.
- **Do** include a skip-to-content link as the first focusable element.
